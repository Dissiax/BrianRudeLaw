A large portion of my clients are in the Construction Industry. I have extensive experience dealing with the unique and specialized legal needs of such businesses that spans all phases of construction related legal transactions and dispute resolution. I represent numerous commercial subcontractors, and also general contractors, owners, manufacturers and design professionals on both public and private construction projects.

Some of the main areas of my experience are with:

  • Mechanics' Liens
  • Mechanics' Liens
  • Bond Claims/Surety Law
  • Construction Contract Negotiations & Drafting (including AIA, AGC and other common forms)
  • Subcontractor and Material Supply Agreements
  • Construction Disputes
  • Construction Litigation, Arbitration & Mediation
  • Foreclosures
  • Delay & Impact Claims
  • Product Liability Claims
  • Architect & Engineer Design Liability
  • Change Orders, Differing Site Conditions & Delays
  • Construction Defects
  • Payment Issues
  • Construction Defaults & Terminations

 

I am a member of the Minnesota State Bar Association's Construction Law Section and am a long time member of its Legislative Subcommittee.
